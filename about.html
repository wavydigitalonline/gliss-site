About

We make businesses easier to choose.

Most agencies sell services.
We remove friction.

Friction happens when nobody cared enough.
A confusing page. A slow reply. A journey that asks too much.

GLISS exists to find those moments and remove them — so the experience finally matches the quality of the work behind it.

Every decision is judged by one standard:
does this make the business easier to choose.
