About

We make businesses easier to choose.

Most agencies sell services. We remove friction.

A business rarely loses a customer because of bad service. It loses them in the moments no one is watching — an unclear price, a slow reply, a page that asks too much before it gives anything back.

GLISS exists to find those moments and remove them.

Kevin started GLISS after years of watching good businesses struggle. Not from lack of ambition. From friction no one had named yet.

Every decision we make is judged by one standard: does this make the business easier to choose.

Meet the Founder

Built from the ground up.

I'm Kevin, the founder of Gliss.

I didn't start this with funding, an office, or expensive equipment. I started it on a Xiaomi Redmi phone while working 10-hour shifts, building websites during breaks and late into the night.

That experience taught me something most agencies never have to learn: every click matters, every enquiry matters, and every rand a business invests should create measurable value.

Gliss was built on one belief: growing a business shouldn't feel complicated. The best digital experiences are smooth, effortless, and almost invisible. When everything works as it should, your customers don't notice the technology — they simply take the next step.

Today that same mindset shapes every project: thoughtful design, practical systems, and technology that disappears so your customers simply take the next step — and choose you.

Smooth · Precise · Intentional
The Philosophy

Design is a form of respect.

When you respect someone's attention, you remove everything that makes them work for an answer. That's not decoration. That's what makes a business easy to choose.
